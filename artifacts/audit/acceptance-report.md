Numerical checks were made on original rows, not the interpolated grid. The audit script independently recalculated signed z-score, absolute score, strict flags, finite values, ordered bands, row IDs, and original timestamp order for all 12 cases. The stable recurrence fixture gave `z=10`, `r=50.918938533204674`, `w=0.01926079438932388`, `h=0.03852158877864776`, and next sigma `0.2` to test tolerance. The revised segment fixture produced the values specified above. These are meaningful but do not exhaust every edge case listed in the independent-review assignment.

All four original series (4600, 4020, 6336, 9216 rows) ran under all three models. With BLAS threads set to one, repeat-call medians in `../benchmark.json` range from about 17–51 ms for AR, 24–50 ms for Stable AR and 119–214 ms for seasonal. Cold *process wall time*, which includes importing Python libraries, ranged about 1.3–1.9 s. This machine-specific result meets the stated ~2 s interaction aim for these cases, but is not a production SLO or a comparison to the paper's capacity figures.

Browser screenshots capture ordinary AR at 4020 points, 263 flags, 188 intervals and `OK` at 16.7%, and seasonal at 4020 points, 2685 flags, 261 intervals and `ACTIVE` at 83.3%. The latter shows the >30% model-fit warning. `reference-interval-ui.png` captures the later display-only approximate 95% normal-reference interval in green, distinct from the blue anomaly threshold band. The browser showed observed/expected curves, filled ranges, markers, score chart, alert window, and the data/export controls. Switching seasonal to AR hid the prior results until rerun. Both CSV and JSON buttons raised download events. Streamlit AppTest verified 120-row labeled upload and that malformed input removes old metrics, but the in-app browser's file chooser timed out twice. Downloaded byte content was not available from the browser automation API; the library's `to_dict()` was strict-JSON checked, and the CLI generated a 4020-point smoke output.

No source example has labels, so the app makes no quality claim from those data. The synthetic level-shift fixture did **not** show a Stable AR advantage: a contaminated initial OLS fit left late-shift median scores around 0.61 for Stable AR and 0.99 for AR, with zero threshold-3 flags in either case. Seasonal scale varied by phase in the fixed-seed fixture; a missing peak scored about 45.6. On one real series seasonal flags 67% of observations at the default period, indicating a poor model fit rather than proven anomaly prevalence. The UI warns at >30%.

No P0/P1/P2 implementation defect was confirmed in this audit. Browser upload and downloaded-byte parsing remain acceptance gaps, and independent review by a different agent remains outstanding. See `repair-task.md` for exact follow-up steps. The app is a local retrospective explorer; no production monitoring, delivery, or published benchmark reproduction was tested.
